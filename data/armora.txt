# Armora

Armora is an AI-powered CRM and business automation platform built for businesses in Saudi Arabia and the Middle East.

It transforms WhatsApp, customer communication channels, and business workflows into a unified intelligent management system.

## What Armora Does

Armora helps businesses:

* Manage customer conversations from a single platform.
* Allow multiple employees to work on one WhatsApp business number.
* Automate customer support using AI agents.
* Handle bookings, reservations, and appointments.
* Manage customer relationships and sales pipelines.
* Automate internal workflows and business processes.
* Analyze customer interactions and business performance.
* Create company-specific AI assistants with custom personalities and knowledge.

## Key Features

### AI Customer Service Agents

Businesses can create AI agents trained on:

* Company information
* Product catalogs
* Service policies
* Internal documentation
* Frequently asked questions

The AI agent can respond using the company's preferred tone, style, and branding.

### WhatsApp CRM

Armora turns WhatsApp into a complete CRM platform.

Features include:

* Shared inbox
* Multi-agent support
* Customer history
* Automated replies
* Lead management
* Team collaboration

### Booking and Reservation Management

Businesses can manage:

* Appointments
* Reservations
* Customer scheduling
* Availability management
* Automated reminders

### Business Workflow Automation

Armora automates repetitive tasks such as:

* Lead assignment
* Customer follow-ups
* Support ticket routing
* Appointment confirmations
* Internal notifications

### AI Knowledge System

Armora can connect company knowledge and documents into an AI-powered knowledge assistant that can answer customer or employee questions.

## Target Industries

## Technical Architecture

Armora is built using:

* Golang backend infrastructure
* Flutter cross-platform applications
* PostgreSQL database systems
* Cloud-native architecture
* AI and Large Language Model integrations

## Regional Focus

Armora is optimized for:

* Saudi Arabia
* GCC countries
* Arabic language support
* Gulf business workflows
* Regional compliance and data requirements

## Positioning

Armora is an AI-powered WhatsApp CRM and business automation platform that combines customer communication, AI agents, workflow automation, bookings, and business management in a single system.

Its goal is to help organizations reduce manual work, improve customer experience, and operate with AI-enhanced workflows.
